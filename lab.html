Jerusalem Neuroeconomics Lab

Understanding the cognitive foundations of economic decision-making

The lab combines economic models, behavioral experiments, and cognitive neuroscience methods to study strategic reasoning, valuation, risk, social adaptation, and choice inconsistency.

Visit the lab's website to learn more about our research, and meet the team.
